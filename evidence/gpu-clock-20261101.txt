## The GPU clock on the EVB10: what the clock service does to it, what it really runs at against the voltage, and the GPU's busy share (runs gs1, gv1, gw1; serial logs of the kernel)
# gs1: cd uefi-evb10 && EXTRA_KERNEL_ARGS="rk3588-dvfs=8" CAPTURE_SECS=110 EARLY_STOP=0 tools/xnu-probe/run-kernel.sh gs1   (the rail raised to 850 mV, each rate of the clock service in turn, then back)
# gv1: EXTRA_KERNEL_ARGS="rk3588-dvfs=10": for each setting the rail walks down from 850 mV in 12.5 mV steps to 675 mV
# gw1: EXTRA_KERNEL_ARGS="rk3588-dvfs=9 evb10-touch-demo=120 mali-timeline=1" CAPTURE_SECS=235: GPU_STATUS and SHADER_READY sampled every ~7 us for 50 ms windows
# "measured" is the GPU's cycle counter (GPU_COMMAND 0x103 starts it) against the architected counter over 5 ms, as the Mali driver's own clock probe does. sel158 is CLKSEL_CON(158): bits 7:5 the
# source (0 GPLL, 1 CPLL), bits 4:0 the divider minus one, and bit 14 (0x4000), which the clock service sets for the settings 300 to 900 MHz: the GPU then runs from its PVTPLL.
# At start the Mali driver's setting is CPLL / 2 = 750 MHz at 750 mV (the firmware's rail voltage). The service ignores 1000 MHz (it reads 750 MHz afterwards). 800 and 900 are one ring (same numbers).
