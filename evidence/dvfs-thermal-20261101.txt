## Temperature sensors (TSADC) on the EVB10: bring-up, an idle comparison with and without the governor, and the over-temperature limit (runs tb1, tg1, gt33; serial logs of the kernel)
# tb1: cd uefi-evb10 && EXTRA_KERNEL_ARGS="rk3588-dvfs=7" CAPTURE_SECS=600 EARLY_STOP=0 tools/xnu-probe/run-kernel.sh tb1   (the governor only watches: the clocks stay where the firmware left them)
# tg1: the same with EXTRA_KERNEL_ARGS="rk3588-dvfs=6" (the governor)
# gt33: CAPTURE_SECS=235, EXTRA_KERNEL_ARGS="evb10-touch-demo=120 mali-timeline=1 rk3588-dvfs-tmax=33" (the limit set just above the idle temperature, so that the scripted swipes reach it)
# The firmware leaves the TSADC clocked and out of reset but not sampling (AUTO_CON = 0); the kernel switches the sampling on and reads the seven channels (soc, the two A76 pairs, the A55 cores,
# the SoC centre, the GPU, the NPU). One code step is 0.93 C. The board has a heat sink; the idle temperature is 30 C and the difference between the two idle runs (0.4 C) is smaller than the
# difference between their starting temperatures (31.4 and 30.5 C), so temperature does not resolve what the governor saves: the log carries a model of the cores power instead.
# (The kernel of tb1 and tg1 had a touch boost default of 1000 ms and no GPU governor; that of gt33 had the GPU governor too, and the same boost default.)
