## What these lines show
# 27.895  one device answers on I2C7, 0x11; all 53 registers read; the write check passes.
# 27.959  power domain 38 (audio) is on at the hand-over (repair status bit 19), so the I2S registers can be read.
# 27.959  GPLL 1188 MHz / 4 = 297 MHz, fraction 512/12375: 12.288 MHz (256 x 48 kHz).
# 27.967  SYS_GRF 0x318 bit 0 (MCLK to the pin) cleared; the pins were already routed as wanted (IOC lines show the same value before and after).
# 28.020  I2S0 as the master: CKR TRCM = 1 (transmit-only sync), 64 BCLK per LRCK (0x3f); CLKDIV 4 (BCLK 3.072 MHz).
# 30.023  transmit 96026 words in 2.000 s = 48012 words/s, INTSR 0 (no underrun, no overrun); receive 95998 words = 47998 words/s.
#         The receive samples vary within a small range (not all zero, not the transmitted sine): the codec, a slave, is clocked and sends ADC data.
# Not observed: any sound (the headphone / speaker enable GPIOs are not driven; no person at the board).
